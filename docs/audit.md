# Audit: velora.se today, and the benchmarks

Reviewed 2026-11-02 at mobile width (375 px) and on desktop. Facts are in `facts.md`.

## 1. The current start page

### Section order today
Hero (3 check-mark claims) → what it is / results → "Vi är experter" → weight calculator → "3 enkla steg" →
"Alltid med Velora" → patient stories → "Vad du får" → experts → "Visste du att" → pricing →
"Byt till en bättre vårdgivare" → FAQ.

The visitor gets claims about results and medicine before they get proof (people, team, credentials).
The page asks for trust before it has earned it.

### What doesn't work (your observations, confirmed)
1. **No action in the hero.** On mobile the first screen holds a headline, three claims and an image. The only action is "Kom igång" in the navbar.
2. **Patient stories come too late.** They sit after the results and method claims, about two-thirds of the way down the page.
3. **The calculator** shows one number (e.g. "−21 kg"). The premise is in a footnote ("16–25 % … första året"), and there's no time period next to the number and no explanation of what it depends on.
4. **Card modules** ("3 enkla steg", "Visste du att", "Vad du får") are text cards with no images. They list facts instead of telling the journey.
5. **"Alltid med Velora" and "Byt till en bättre vårdgivare" repeat each other**: both are lists of selling points (online, licensed doctors, guarantee).
6. **Hero action hypothesis:** "Boka gratis bedömningssamtal" already exists on the site, mid-page and in the FAQ. It names what you get (free, a call, an assessment), whereas "Kom igång" doesn't.

### Additional findings
7. **Five different action labels:** "Kom igång", "Se om du kvalificerar dig", "Boka gratis bedömningssamtal", "Byt idag", "Kom igång idag". All five go to the same place (/kom-igang).
8. **The action promises a booking, but the flow starts with a questionnaire.** /kom-igang opens with "Svara på några frågor…". The text under the button should set that expectation, otherwise the first click feels like a bait-and-switch.
9. **The tone swings between calm and ad-like.** Examples: "Nytt vetenskapligt genombrott", "ingen hunger & inget sug", exclamation marks ("så som det alltid borde vara!"), "Högst patientnöjdhet på marknaden".
10. **Medicine-first.** Drug brand names and kg-per-week numbers carry the story. This is risky under the advertising rules (see `direction.md` §3), and it makes Velora look like a pill shop instead of a care provider.
11. **Fear-based statistics** ("13 olika cancerformer", "5,3 miljoner i riskzonen") pull against "understood, not sold to".
12. **The start page and /om-oss contradict each other**: 3 steps vs 4 steps, and two different voices. **/om-oss is already close to the direction we want.** It's calm and concrete, has trust blocks (IVO, licensed staff, records and data protection, "Velora säljer inte läkemedel"), and uses "Du binder dig inte till något". The start page hasn't caught up with it.
13. **Pink wash plus a heavy rounded sans for headings** reads as friendly and app-like rather than premium. Colour is used everywhere, so it no longer signals anything.

### What to keep (it already feels like Velora)
- The plum colour family (`#751A4B` → `#2C0015`) and the lowercase wordmark. The logo is up for change, so we don't touch it.
- Warm, everyday photography of real people. No clinical imagery.
- The core message: "övervikt handlar inte om dålig motivation eller lathet".
- Named clinicians with their titles.
- Honest pricing that spells out the extra costs (health check, medicine), plus the guarantee.
- The voice and trust blocks on /om-oss.
- The FAQ, which already answers the real objections (side effects, cost, eligibility).

## 2. Benchmarks: what to borrow

Relivo and Tandem were reviewed live on mobile. Oura and Apple Health are based on your notes and general knowledge of both sites. **They have not been re-checked in this session.**

| Benchmark | What you saw | Pattern to borrow for Velora | Don't copy |
|-----------|--------------|------------------------------|------------|
| **Oura** | Iconic, premium, reassuring | Restraint: few elements, generous white space, editorial typography, science told calmly with one point per screen. The product appears as part of everyday life. | Tech-gadget coolness; health care needs warmth. |
| **Relivo** | Iconic, premium | Full-bleed warm photo with a short headline and **the action inside the hero**. A short value line. A visible note next to claims ("Se godkända hälsopåståenden"), which is a model for showing premises. **Its deep maroon is close to Velora's plum, which shows that keeping the plum can still feel premium.** | E-commerce pushiness ("Du sparar 622 kr"), price anchoring, scrolling tickers. |
| **Tandem** | Calm tone, a concrete service | Says plainly what the service does, step by step. Clinicians are named with titles. A calm, factual security and compliance section (certifications as quiet facts). | B2B language, investor news in the hero. |
| **Apple Health** | Privacy and trust | Privacy presented **as a benefit**, in plain sentences with simple icons. Velora's privacy policy supports this directly (`facts.md`, Privacy). | Product-feature density. |

### Combined, for Velora
Relivo's hero structure, Oura's restraint and pacing, Tandem's concreteness and named experts, and Apple's way of presenting privacy, all carried by **Velora's own plum, wordmark and photography**.
